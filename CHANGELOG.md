# Changelog

Versions go 0.1.0, 0.1.1 ... 0.1.9, then 0.2.0. Each release is a GitHub pre-release with a Setup and a Portable download.
New entries go under "Unreleased" as work lands; a release moves them under its version.

## Unreleased

## 0.1.8 (18 Nov 2026)
- Automation: sleep, hibernate, shut down, restart and sign out work again from a hotkey, a mouse button, Run now, another PC and a rule you start with its own key; only a rule started by a trigger is held back, within 2 minutes of OmniControl starting or the PC waking (measured so a clock change cannot stretch it). Sleep and hibernate switch on Windows' shut-down permission first, and a refused shut down, restart or sign out is now reported.
- OSD: a smart device's state change (a command, a read that finds it switched, from a hotkey, rule, tray or another PC) shows on the OSD with the icon picked for the device, its name and On or Off; a failed switch shows in red. One new "Smart devices" switch under Show in OSD, on by default.
- Other PCs: the Now playing card's middle pill shows Pause while it plays and Play while paused, and a small "Playing" / "Paused" tag sits at the right of the title line (none when nothing plays).
- Displays: monitors missing or half-read after Remote Desktop, unlock, wake, log-on or at start are found again without Refresh. The automatic tries now wait until every monitor has actually answered (before, a try counted as "found" as soon as the monitors were opened, so one try at 2 s ended it while DDC/CI was still asleep), compare against the displays Windows lists at that moment rather than the last look, and run at start too (2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 s). A monitor arriving or leaving, the screens coming back on and a wake without a message are now listened for; the periodic check (now every minute by default) refreshes when the displays changed or a monitor answers less than it did. A display mirroring two monitors and a failed laptop-panel read no longer drop monitors from the list. Every automatic refresh is one line in hardware-log.txt with what started it and what it found.

## 0.1.7 (17 Nov 2026)
- Automation: "When the PC wakes from sleep" now comes from a power journal (sleep, wake, lock, log-in, shut down, Modern Standby display off/on and a clock check, in power-journal.json and power-log.txt) and fires once per real wake, confirmed by two sources; "At log in" fires once per sign-in. After a wake or log-in, a smart-device step waits for the network and the device to answer, is tried again up to 3 times, and the rule's tag says honestly how it went ("Ran 01:36 after 2 tries", "Failed: Orbit 11 not reachable"). "Needs Start with Windows" has a "Turn on Start with Windows" link.
- Displays: a rotation (or a hotkey) now finds its screen again by the monitor's device path each time it runs, so after a replug, driver reset or GPU switch renumbers the screens it never turns the wrong one; a screen that is gone is skipped with one plain sentence and a log line.
- Displays: screen power and "Turn off displays" are now On / Off / Toggle families (new Toggle and On actions; the Off actions keep their ids and keys); a screen already in the asked state is left alone.
- Displays: a rotation now sticks across sleep, wake, sign-in and restart: besides the old registry write it is saved to Windows' display database the way Settings does (before, Windows could restore an older stored turn on wake, flipping Portrait). The position is kept explicitly, and every rotation is one line in display-log.txt with the reason (Orientation card or a hotkey/action).
- Set to state for more than smart devices: Mute, Microphone mute, Master FX, Keep awake, Hotkeys, Warm light at night, App autoscroll block and Pause in full-screen games now each have On, Off and Toggle as one grouped row (Direction setting); the Toggle actions keep their ids and keys. On or Off does nothing when the thing is already there.
- Other PCs: every action or state that runs on another PC is marked "Other PCs · name" in bold accent, with the PC's real name muted after it when it has a nickname (the action window, Actions and hotkeys, and the app search; a rename follows at once).
- Displays: after a Remote Desktop session (or unlock, logon, wake) the monitors are found again by themselves: the same refresh as the Refresh button is tried at 2, 5, 10 and 20 s until the monitors that were there answer again, instead of once after 2 s when DDC/CI is often not ready yet. Remote disconnect and logon are now listened for too, .NET's SystemEvents is a second listener (the same event told twice counts once), and every announcement and try is one line in hardware-log.txt.
- "On" and "Off" actions of a smart device (also "via" a method) now set the device to a state instead of just re-running the method: when the device is already in that state nothing is sent (the status line says so), so a Simulation method no longer flips it back; an unknown state is read again first when it can be, and sent anyway when it cannot.
- Knobs: a click or double-click on the value box under a knob opens it for typing and never resets the knob (a double-click on the knob face still resets).
- Save dialog: a folder you save to becomes the folder offered next time (the action's "Save folder"), even without "Always save here"; the tick only skips the dialog.
- Screen recording works on more PCs and can no longer hang: it tries the graphics card's encoder with the graphics-card screen copy, then a graphics card's encoder (another adapter's when there is one) with the GDI copy, then the software encoder; a way that does not open, encodes no frame within 4 s or stops answering is dropped and the next one records (said in the status line), a Stop whose file does not finish within 10 s ends as failed instead of hanging, and the way that worked is remembered for this PC until its graphics drivers change. MP4 is held to 60 fps. Nothing of the recorder runs on the UI thread any more (sound switches included). Every recording writes its steps to errors-log.txt (adapters, encoder, formats, first frame, frame counts, each Stop step with its time, any HRESULT); a failed recording shows the log path and "Copy details".
- Screen recording: Stop no longer waits for the screen copy and sound to be released before the recording counts as saved (the file is complete as soon as Media Foundation has finished it); the video now starts when the encoder is ready instead of with a second-long gap while the graphics card's encoder opens; a locked screen, a UAC prompt or Ctrl+Alt+Del no longer makes a graphics-card recording fail (Windows refuses the screen copy there, which was not caught): the area is copied with GDI until it is back. New tests record the screen for real (MP4 hardware and software, GIF) and check the files.
- Save dialog for screenshots and recordings ("Save screenshot" / "Save recording"): name, folder with Browse… and "Always save here, don't ask"; the folder and the choice are the action's own "Save folder" and new "Ask where to save" settings (on by default). Discard asks first.
- Recording bar: X, Esc twice and the emergency key ask "Discard this recording?" first; a Stop that has not finished within 8 seconds is no longer thrown away by itself: it is said, and the question offers "Force close".
- The "Screenshot saved" / "Video saved" pop-up shows the folder as a third muted line, shortened in the middle.
- Screenshots are saved and copied as JPG at 80 % by default (fresh install and Restore defaults); PNG and the others stay selectable. Copy puts the picture on the clipboard as a bitmap plus JPG ("JFIF"), or plus PNG when PNG is chosen.
- Other PCs: Ctrl+T opens a New PC tab and Ctrl+W closes the open one (bindable actions "New PC tab" and "Close PC tab", acting only while the page is open); closing an unfinished New PC tab or unpairing a PC asks "Yes / No / Cancel"; every Yes/No dialog takes Y, N and Esc (letters underlined).
- Now playing media card on two lines: the track info on top, the previous, play or pause and next buttons below as three centred icon-only pills with their names in tooltips.
- Other PCs knob cards: the device chip takes the room between the name and the knob and wraps onto up to two lines before it trims.
- Other PCs: the screens' brightness is one "Brightness" knob card with a display chip ("All displays" or one display, remembered per PC).
- Other PCs: mirrored slider cards (brightness, master volume...) show the master knob with the value under it and a two-line name; every knob can also be right-clicked to type its value.
- Closed group headers show the muted settings count everywhere: Mouse Buttons, the Gestures Areas group, a display's software picture group and the Other PCs mirrored groups no longer blank the shared count.
- "Pause in full-screen games" (Mouse and Trackpad) is now off by default (fresh install and Restore defaults).
- Cards at any window width: a card's name wraps and is never wider than a set limit, drop-downs and galleries shrink in the room right of the name instead of covering it, a slider's value box keeps its width (all from shared styles and resources).
- Other PCs: New PC tabs drag and reorder like PC tabs; one saved tab order covers both, and a New PC tab keeps its place when it becomes the PC's tab.
- FX window: the display-only feed for plug-ins and the EQ spectrum on Equalizer APO rows is level-matched to the full-volume signal (the loopback comes after the Windows master volume, so lowering the PC volume no longer drops the meters); while the device is muted or under 2 % the display is not fed and the tag says so.
- Other PCs: the "+" opens a "New PC" tab that holds the whole pairing flow and becomes the PC's tab once paired; unfinished New PC tabs are kept (with the way chosen and what was typed) across restarts; the Pair a PC panel above the tabs is gone. A PC's groups sit in two independent columns (one when narrow) and no card is taller than its content. A PC's panel shows at once: while what it shares is still arriving each group shows the loading spinner. PC tab names no longer trim while there is room. Half-width slider cards (Brightness, Master volume) keep the slider and the whole value at any width: the chip trims first, then the slider to its minimum, then the slider and value wrap under the label.
- Screen recording: fixed Stop not stopping. "Record area" started the recording from inside the picture window's own message, so the recording's windows could be made off the UI thread and the overlay was destroyed while still handling the click; the start is now posted to the UI thread and always continues there. New: a Cancel X beside Stop (nothing is saved), Esc twice quickly cancels, a Stop that does not finish within 8 seconds aborts, and a never-blocked "Stop screen recording" action (Ctrl+Alt+F12; again while it is ending = cancel).
- Screenshot drawing: overlapping highlighter strokes (and a stroke crossing itself) no longer get darker; a run of markers is drawn solid into one layer faded once, the same for the screen and the saved file.
- Hardware is looked at again by itself: after a Remote Desktop session (or unlock, wake from sleep, a display, device or sound-device change) the monitors, displays, sound devices, touchpad and smart devices refresh without pressing Refresh. One master list of hardware watches (`HardwareWatch`), one refresh per watch per burst; an action whose picked monitor is missing refreshes it once and retries; new App settings card "Check hardware every" (Off by default; 1, 5 or 15 minutes) as a safety net.
- Sidebar: the dead space at the left edge is cut to a third (about 8 px instead of 25). One shared set of `Nav*` thickness resources in `App.axaml` feeds the nav items and headings (so the preset manager, Devices and other `ListBox.nav` sidebars follow), the title, the update pill and the divider; the page content gets the freed width.
- Tray quick panel: the "Open OmniControl" button's tooltip shows the current keys of the show-or-hide action, e.g. "Open OmniControl (Alt+X)", read live (new shared `Hotkeys/ActionKeyTip`); no key shows just the name.
- FX window: a plug-in that Equalizer APO runs (so its editor copy in OmniControl heard nothing) gets a display-only feed while its editor is open: a loopback of the playing device, processed by the editor copy and thrown away, never played or routed and never counted as processing. One tag in the status strip says "Display fed with what you hear", the tooltip says it may differ from what Equalizer APO does and that dynamics meters read the already-processed sound. Plug-ins in OmniControl's own engine already get the real sound and no feed.
- Parametric equaliser: a live spectrum is drawn behind the curve (a faint filled shape in the muted brush), only while the graph is on screen, worked out off the UI thread at about 15 pictures a second. In OmniControl's own engine it is the real sound entering the equaliser; for the master in Equalizer APO it is what you hear (same tag as above).
- Parametric equaliser graph: the band circles are half their former size (one shared size, `EqBandCircle`); the number is left out (the list and the selected band's info line carry it) and the hit area to grab a point is unchanged.
- Other PCs: the PC list column is now browser-style tabs under the title (icon, name, status dot, route word; drag to reorder, saved; right-click: Give nickname..., Unpair; "+" pairs another). The header line holds the probe tag, Connect and Unpair; the Tailscale checklist shows only when a check fails; a PC's groups are half width, two per row when wide.
- Other PCs list: the status tag is two lines ("online" over "via Tailscale" or "LAN"), so the PC name keeps its width and no longer breaks mid-word.
- Narrow cards: a slider card with a leading chip (Other PCs mirror's Master volume) no longer clips its slider or value; the chip trims its words (full name in its tooltip) first, then the slider shrinks to a least width, and the value and label never clip.
- Other PCs: a paired PC can get a local nickname (right-click it in the list, Give nickname...). The list, the detail header (nickname, with the real name muted beside it), Actions and hotkeys, states and search show it; it is kept only on this PC and never sent to the other one.
- Other PCs: a PC's online tag, its Connect state and the last row of the Tailscale checklist now come from a probe, an authenticated and encrypted ping answered by that PC with a pong that repeats a fresh code, over the chosen route; the measured time is in the tag's tooltip and a failure says why (timed out, refused, authentication failed). A PC no longer shows "via Tailscale" while its route is set to LAN, and an old stored Tailscale address no longer blocks the switch for two minutes. Pairing over the internet with an already paired PC's address no longer starts a second pairing.
- Screenshot overlay is much faster on large and high-refresh screens (4K, 140 Hz): the frozen picture is now shown the way Lightshot shows it, by a plain Windows window that copies only the changed strips from a dimmed and a bright copy made once, so moving, resizing and drawing a selection no longer make the whole 4K screen be cleared, copied and composed again on every frame. The bars, size label and text box float above it as small pop-ups; looks, keys and the export are unchanged.
- Fix: clicking a colour in the screenshot overlay's colour pop-down changes the drawing colour again (the pop-down was closed before the click's command ran, which cut the colour off).
- Other PCs: the detail header, the list tag and the Tailscale checklist now all follow the same probe-verified route state (no more "Tailscale not working yet" beside a passing check), and "Check again" no longer stays pressed: it stays enabled and reads "Checking..." while a check runs.
- Tapo scripts: the account email is always sent in lower case (trimmed), also for edited copies of the Tapo template saved before that setting existed; the typed email is kept as typed.

## 0.1.6 (17 Nov 2026)
- Screen recording is much lighter on large and high-resolution screens: an MP4 with the hardware encoder now keeps its frames on the graphics card (Desktop Duplication straight into the encoder, colour conversion there too) instead of copying every frame through memory; MP4 frame buffers are reused instead of made anew each frame; a GIF is encoded on its own thread while the next frame is copied. If the graphics-card way is not available the recording works as before.
- Fixed a crash when a remove badge appeared (a search box with text, tray panel edit): its round corner read a size instead of a radius.
- FX window: FX > Save chain... (Ctrl+S) and Load chain... (Ctrl+O) keep the whole chain (effects with all settings, device folders and their Mix) in one .omnifx file and replace the window's chain from one; a file that is not a chain gives one plain sentence and leaves the chain as it was. Works in the master and every app's FX window.
- Fix: a device folder's "Change in Mixer" opens the Mixer at that device's own row when it has one, else at the master row.
- FX window: device folders. A "Device folder" button (speaker with a plus) and the Add effect window's first entry make a folder for one playback device: a chain row with chevron, on tick, glyph (accent while it plays, cable blue on a cable, muted otherwise), name over its method tag, effect count; its effects sit under it with an accent bar and run only while that device plays (effects outside every folder say "all devices"). The folder's editor shows the device menu (both method tags), the device's method read only with "Change in Mixer", Mix, its effects and "Add effect here...". The Add effect window got the master foot. Folders can be saved as presets ("Save folder as preset..." and the preset manager's "Device folders" group: device, Mix and effects; loading one replaces the selected folder's effects and Mix). "Change in Mixer" opens the Volume page's Mixer at the master row. Mix is applied by OmniControl's own engine; Equalizer APO has no mix, so below 100 % it runs none of the folder's effects and says so. Removing a folder that holds effects asks first; dropping a folder never splits another; the Volume list does not count folders as effects; Up/Down skip a closed folder's rows and Left/Right close or open it.
- Fix: tool tips with nothing in them (empty notes, reasons or summaries) no longer pop up anywhere; one shared rule drops any empty tip.
- Other PCs over the internet (Tailscale): the Pair a PC panel has two pills, On this network and Over the internet. Over the internet shows a read-only Tailscale checklist (installed and version, running, signed in, connected and this PC's address, OmniControl allowed through Windows Firewall on Tailscale, other PC reachable), each failing line with one fix (Get Tailscale, Start Tailscale, Sign in, Allow = Windows' own administrator prompt) and a Check again pill; pick the other PC from the online PCs of your tailnet or type its name or address, then the same code and Pair. Pairing is two-way: both PCs keep each other's Tailscale address, so either can dial. A paired PC shows "via Tailscale" only while its link really runs over Tailscale. Pairing PCs of this version with an older version no longer works (the pairing exchange carries the addresses).
- Other PCs: choosing "Connect: Tailscale" on a PC already paired (over LAN) no longer claims "via Tailscale" or shows a pairing error. The row's tags show the real route (online · LAN until a Tailscale link works), and the PC's detail shows "Set up Tailscale for this PC": the same checklist as Pair a PC over the internet plus "<PC> on your Tailscale" (found among your tailnet's PCs by name; fix "How to add it") and "Reaches OmniControl on it", with Check again. No new pairing code: when every row is ticked the PC's Tailscale address is kept, ours is sent over the current link, and both PCs switch to Tailscale by themselves. If Tailscale later stops, the checklist comes back.
- Effects in Equalizer APO now stay applied: OmniControl watches Equalizer APO's config folder and reads it back, so when Equalizer APO's own Configuration Editor (or another program) writes the config without OmniControl's line, the line is put back at once (no more toggling effects to recover). It also re-checks after a device change and after sleep, asks Equalizer APO to reload after a burst of edits, and says once if another program keeps taking the line out. New switch "Keep OmniControl's effects in place" (on by default; FX window Options and App settings > Plug-ins; also a state): off leaves the config as the other program wrote it, the row says so and the FX window offers "Put back". The FX window shows a tag when Equalizer APO's config also runs effects of its own.
- Fix: OmniControl's include line is always at the top of Equalizer APO's config (a line found lower down, or twice, is moved), a failed clean-up of a locked config folder is retried every few seconds instead of on every change, an IR Loader's Auto level is measured off the screen's thread, an IR file replaced under the same name is loaded again by both engines, and an IR file that can't be read or is missing is said on the row instead of passing as running.
- OSD: a Position menu with your own named places ("Custom position..." opens a placement window: screen, a 3 x 3 anchor, two offsets, a name, with the OSD shown live and draggable; named places are presets, "Manage places..." opens the preset manager), and a Show on card (All screens by default, the screen with the mouse, or ticked screens). Fix: an open drop-down is drawn above the cards below it.
- Display brightness up/down now have suggested keys (Ctrl+Shift+Up / Ctrl+Shift+Down), offered by "Suggest"; not bound by default.
- Fix: "Suggest" in the Actions and hotkeys window no longer adds and replaces a temporary key box while giving the key.
- Quick panel: the action is now "Show or hide quick panel" (default Alt+Z), with an optional setting "Use for Win+A (replaces Windows' quick settings)", off by default.
- New action "Show or hide OmniControl" (default key Alt+X, works even with hotkeys switched off): hides the window to the tray when it is in front, otherwise shows and focuses it.
- Smart Devices: "Import from another PC" (a group under the devices, and an Import pill / link beside Add device): tick devices another paired PC has and copy their set-ups here. Passwords and account emails are never sent (type the sign-in again); your own script templates only travel when "Include scripts" is on. Devices already here are dimmed.
- Actions and hotkeys window: a "Hotkeys" On/Off switch at the left of its top bar pauses every keyboard hotkey (keys kept; mouse buttons, gestures and rules keep working; kept between runs). Also a "Hotkeys on" state and a "Hotkeys: on/off" action.
- Restore defaults in the Actions and hotkeys window now leaves the Displays "Suggested keys" switch on with its keys, as on a fresh install.
- Displays: each Orientation card has a "Suggested keys" switch (Ctrl+Alt+Up Landscape, Down Landscape flipped, Left Portrait, Right Portrait flipped). A fresh install starts with it on; if you already have your own keys on those actions it starts off. Turning it on remembers your own keys and turning it off puts them back; changing any of those keys by hand turns it off; a key another action has is skipped and shown as "Also on: ...". Kept per shortcut set.
- Scrolling over the taskbar changes the volume by default (new users and Restore defaults; a saved choice is kept).
- Every search box has a small clear X inside its right edge (only while it has text); Esc clears it first.
- Screenshot overlay no longer lags on 4K or several monitors: the picture and the dim are drawn once, the bright selection is a clipped layer, so selecting, moving and resizing only move a clip and the thin frame; finished drawings are drawn again only when one is added or undone, and the drawing in progress alone while you draw. The overlay also opens faster and holds half the memory per monitor (one copy of each screen instead of two).
- GIF recording encodes several times faster (a lighter compression table), so large areas drop fewer frames.

- Typeable values (every number you can click or double-click to type) now look like a faint field box with an accent edge and I-beam on hover; the Parametric EQ's overall gain is typeable too, and typed gains keep two decimals (7.25 dB). The EQ's band circles are 22 px, the selected band shows two drag handles for its bandwidth (double-click one to reset), thick sliders show grip lines on the knob, and the FX window's effect list has a draggable splitter whose width is remembered.
- New page Other PCs (LAN, basic only; Windows Remote Desktop is for anything more): pair another PC with a 6-digit code and see and control its basics from here, with icons: Screens (each screen's power and brightness, an all switch), Sound (output, volume, mute, microphone mute), Now playing (what plays, previous, play/pause, next), Smart devices (a switch per device), Power (battery, Keep awake, Lock, Sign out, Sleep, Restart, Shut down; Restart and Shut down ask first). That set is shared with every paired PC; there is nothing to choose. An offline PC shows "last seen"; its items stay in hotkeys, the mouse, gestures, rules and search under "Other PCs", unavailable until it is back, and go when it is unpaired.
- Other PCs now follows its design: Sound's output is a chip inside the volume slider's row, Now playing is one card (title over artist and app, Previous, Play/Pause in the accent while it plays, Next), and the pickers list a section's groups by name.
- New: Now playing state and Play or pause, Next track and Previous track actions (Windows' current media session); Keep awake state and Toggle keep awake action (the PC does not sleep by itself while on; screens may still turn off).

- Displays: new action "Turn off displays" (Hardware group, Power section; also a default tray tile for tray layouts you have not edited) switches every screen off after a one-second pause; any mouse or key input wakes them, as Windows does.
- Smart Devices: a Simulation method row now has an emulator chip ("Emulator: running · 1.9 GB" or "Emulator: closed"). A click starts it (hidden) or closes it; a right-click opens the Keep running menu with Close now / Start and Show at its top. The emulator's running state is also in the master list of states.
- Microphone mute: new setting "Mute the way your keyboard does" on the Toggle microphone mute action. Press your keyboard's microphone mute key once and muting sends that key, so laptops whose mute light only follows their own key light up; after a short wait the real mute is checked and Windows' own mute is used if the key changed nothing. A keyboard whose key can't be copied says so in one line and keeps the normal mute.
- Tray: the master and microphone mute tiles (and the volume) now follow Windows' own change notification, the same one the volume pop-up uses, so they update at once instead of at the next scan.
- Tray: the small mark on a tile now comes only from its state's own "being checked / failed / last known" status (one shared path), and the microphone mute shows its grey dot while its mute is being checked.


- The icon picker searches faster: a search index built once, matching off the UI thread (an older search is cancelled), cells kept while you type, and the first screenful shown before the rest.

## 0.1.5 (17 Nov 2026)

- Fix: the "Turn off Windows' own screenshot keys" row has its short label, a muted note line under it and a yellow "applies after sign-out" tag while the Win+Shift+S change is waiting for Explorer to restart.
- New: power plans as actions. In the Power section, "+ Add power plan" lists the Windows plans not added yet; each added plan is a "Switch to <plan>" action (tag "active" on the one Windows has active, keys like any action, Remove). Its settings hold that plan's own Windows settings (Display, Sleep, Processor...) for plugged in and on battery, changed in Windows at once, with "Restore plan defaults" for the plans Windows ships.
- New: a switch "Turn off Windows' own screenshot keys" in the screenshot actions' settings (Actions and hotkeys window), off by default, also a searchable action. On: Print Screen no longer opens Snipping Tool (at once) and Win+Shift+S is released (after you sign out). Off, or Restore defaults: both are put back as they were.

- Fixed: clicking the taskbar button sometimes showed nothing when several OmniControl windows were open. Only the main window now has a taskbar button (one `ShowInTaskbar=False` in the base `Window` style); the Actions and hotkeys window and every other window no longer add taskbar entries that Windows could activate while hidden.
- Changed: the round X remove/delete pill (Smart Devices name box, Automation rows, hotkey keys, Mouse action lists, Volume wiring) is now 24 px with a 12 px cross.
- Fixed: a "Something went wrong" message after closing the tray panel quickly, or after stopping a simulation recording.
- Smart Devices: while a method runs, hovering or focusing its power button turns the spinner into a stop square (tooltip "Stop"); a click, Enter or Space stops that method's attempt (a script or adb process we started is killed) and the "Last command" line reads "Stopped", never a failure.
- Smart Devices simulation, hidden emulator: there is no headless mode any more. A hidden emulator starts normally with full graphics and only its window is hidden; Show un-hides it at once (no restart, no question). While a device's set-up is open the window is shown, and it is hidden again when the set-up closes.
- Smart Devices: each method row has a small round power button at its far right. It shows the device's state (accent = on, grey = off), a spinner while that method runs and a red warning (reason on hover) if that method failed; clicking it switches the device through that method only, with no fallback, and the result is the device's "Last command" line.
- Smart Devices simulation, hidden emulator: its screen is woken and unlocked before every run, reading and teaching, so the screenshot is no longer black. When a screenshot is missing, one flat colour or the wrong size, the log says so and the state is read from the switch's attributes and words alone.
- Actions and hotkeys window and every Choose-the-action list: the directions of one thing are one row (Pointer speed up/down, Volume up/down, Brightness up/down, Next/Previous pointer scheme, a smart device's On/Off/Toggle...), with a short key summary on it. In the Actions window the row's settings show one key block per direction, each with its own Run now; in a pick (mouse button, gesture, rule, tray) a "Direction" setting at the top chooses which one is used. Search finds each direction by its own name and opens its row with that direction chosen. Every direction keeps its own id, keys and bindings, and the pages and app-wide search are unchanged.
- Smart Devices: the On / Off / Toggle / Show emulator pills are removed from a ready device's detail; the actions stay registered for hotkeys, rules and the tray.
- New setting "Groups when a page opens" (Always open, Always collapsed, Remember last state; default Remember): a chip "Groups: ..." at the sidebar foot replaces the "Keyboard shortcuts" button (F1 and the new action "Keyboard shortcuts" in search and the Actions and hotkeys window still open the list), and the same choice is a card in App settings > Appearance, included in Restore defaults.
- Update check (App settings > Updates and about): looks at GitHub's release list (pre-releases included) once a day while "Check once a day" is on (default), and on "Check now" or the new action "Check for updates". It only checks; nothing is downloaded. A newer version shows as "<version> available" with "Open on GitHub", and as "Update available: <version>" at the sidebar's foot, which opens that group.
- App settings is now five groups (Appearance; Start and close; Your data; Plug-ins; Updates and about) like the other pages. Theme and Accent color sit side by side; Confirmations moved into Your data; a closed group's header lists the settings inside it.
- The tray panel's edit-mode remove crosses are now a small round badge (tile corner, slider row end); Volume and cable rows' remove uses the same badge.

- Icon library grown from 103 to 523 Tabler icons (cars and transport, finance, nature, weather, commands, chat, devices, pointers, printers, hardware, food and more), each with a category and synonym keywords; the icon picker groups All icons under category headings and finds icons by name, keywords and category.
